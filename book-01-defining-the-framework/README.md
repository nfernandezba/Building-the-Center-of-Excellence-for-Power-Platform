# Book 01 — Defining the Framework Structure for the Power Platform Center of Excellence
### *Definiendo la Estructura Marco para el Centro de Excelencia de Power Platform*
*Foundations and Keys to a Scalable and Successful Model · Fundamentos y Claves para un Modelo Escalable y Exitoso*

---

## About this Book / Sobre este Libro

Any organization with Microsoft licenses will eventually need to ask itself how to govern, adopt, and scale Power Platform responsibly. This book provides the structural foundation to answer that question.

From the fundamentals of what a Center of Excellence is and how it connects to IT governance, to the practical pillars of governance, adoption, innovation, and sustainability — this first volume in the series defines the framework that makes a Power Platform CoE scalable and successful over time.

*Toda organización con licencias de Microsoft deberá, tarde o temprano, plantearse cómo gobernar, adoptar y escalar Power Platform de forma responsable. Este libro proporciona la base estructural para responder esa pregunta.*

*Desde los fundamentos de qué es un Centro de Excelencia y cómo se conecta con la gobernanza de TI, hasta los pilares prácticos de gobernanza, adopción, innovación y sostenibilidad — este primer volumen de la serie define el marco que hace que un CoE de Power Platform sea escalable y exitoso a lo largo del tiempo.*

---

## What You'll Find Inside / Qué Encontrarás

- Introduction to Power Platform and the CoE concept
- IT Center of Excellence models and their interconnection
- Governance pillars and operational compliance
- Building a sustainable adoption model
- Driving innovation with Power Platform
- Strategic leadership and long-term sustainability
- How to bring all the pieces together into a cohesive model
